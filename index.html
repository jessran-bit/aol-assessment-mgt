Five-point rubric scale

Each trait keeps its three written descriptors. Levels 4 and 2 sit between them.

For Table 5-1, levels 3, 4 and 5 count as "meets or exceeds expectations".

How to score

  1. On Score students, choose your name, then your class.
  2. Pick a student from the list. Read each trait's descriptors and click the level that matches the student's work.
  3. Scores save as you click. Click a chosen level again to clear it.
  4. Use Next student to move down the list. A green dot means every trait is scored; orange means partly scored.
  5. Score each student's own work. For group outputs, score the section the student wrote or the individual evaluation, not the group grade.
  6. Wait until the top of the page says "All changes saved" before closing it.
  7. When the class is done, download the Class scoring sheet (all students on one table) and the individual rubric sheets (one page per student), sign them, and send them to the Program Coordinator.

If your class or name is missing, contact the Program Coordinator.
