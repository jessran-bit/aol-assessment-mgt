How to score

  1. Sign in with your own passcode. Your name and classes load automatically. On Score students, choose your class.
  2. Pick a student from the list. Read each trait's descriptors and click the level that matches the student's work.
  3. Scores save as you click. To remove a score, use the Clear link beside the trait.
  4. Use Next student to move down the list. A green dot means every trait is scored; orange means partly scored.
  5. Score each student's own work. For group outputs, score the section the student wrote or the individual evaluation, not the group grade.
  6. Wait until the top of the page says "All changes saved" before closing it.
  7. When every student is scored, click Submit and lock class. Locked scores can no longer be changed; ask your Program Coordinator if a correction is needed.
  8. When the class is done, download the Class scoring sheet (all students on one table) and the individual rubric sheets (one page per student), sign them, and send them to the Program Coordinator.

If your class or name is missing, contact your Program Coordinator.
